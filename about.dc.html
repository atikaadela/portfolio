THE JOURNEY — IN FOUR FRAMES

I didn’t start
in design.

I started by building things with code. Somewhere along the way I became more interested in what happens before the code — how people understand a product, and why some systems feel effortless while others don’t.

I still think like an engineer.

I just design what people experience.
